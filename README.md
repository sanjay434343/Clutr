# Clutr

Clutr makes freeing up storage space on your device fun and effortless! If your phone is running out of space, Clutr deeply scans your local storage to find large media folders, old videos, and duplicated files that are secretly eating up your memory.

Instead of scrolling through a boring, tedious file manager, Clutr uses a fun, gesture-based swiping interface. Just swipe left to trash an unwanted photo or video, and swipe right to keep it! It provides a detailed storage breakdown, beautiful visual charts of your heaviest folders, and automatic duplicate detection. Since it's fully open-source, your files are processed completely offline with absolute privacy. Swipe your way to a cleaner phone today!
